name: studio-production-review
description: Review Hired Guns delivery flow, recurring blockers and rework, and propose measurable workflow improvements. Use for producer reviews or delivery-process diagnosis, not ordinary gameplay implementation or a simple status lookup.
---

Read the canonical [studio policy](../../../docs/studio/POLICY.md), especially roles, local records and continuous process improvement. Do not duplicate or amend its rules inside this skill.

Apply its [model and reasoning routing](../../../docs/studio/POLICY.md#model-and-reasoning-routing) to the current judgement: distinguish established reporting from new dependency/forecast decisions or causal process analysis. Record the task class, requested route and reason in the existing producer record; keep observed execution settings unknown unless exposed. A skill does not switch its running model. Return a bounded routing need to the authorized coordinator when necessary; do not create a worker or resume work merely to change models.

Read the identified production plan, relevant owner statuses/ledgers and bounded evidence. Resolve actual record locations through the configured local registry; a fresh clone may lack those records. Distinguish missing/stale observations from stopped progress and a normal worker wait from an actionable blocker.

Explain which player outcome is delayed, where it waits, who owns the next action and what evidence supports the cause. Use existing measurements; avoid inventing timing, cost or whole-game completion percentages. Reconcile conflicting observations with their owner through authorized channels.

Specifically compare a department's reported active assignment with the actual worker/reviewer state. A finished worker without an owner receipt is a handoff gap; ask the owning coordinator to receive it and record the next receiver. Check that accepting review claims have a valid [review receipt](../../../docs/studio/review-receipts.md); producer records the qualification rather than repairing department evidence or waiving its gate.

Use the [issue workflow](../../../docs/studio/POLICY.md#bugs-issue-reporting-and-triage) for actionable recurring defects. Reuse matching issues and add material evidence rather than opening duplicates. Triage manages severity/routing; producer sets delivery priority and distinguishes the affected blocked work from work that can continue within existing capacity and gates.

For a demonstrated recurring problem, use the [process-change template](../../../docs/studio/templates/process-change.md). Propose one bounded experiment with baseline, success measure and rollback. Identify whether it is routine sequencing within producer authority, a department-owned workflow/tooling change, or a product-owner decision. A recommendation does not dispatch workers, resume schedules or waive acceptance.

Record the result in the producer's existing local plan/ledger, preserving prior evidence. When a trial concludes, recommend adoption, revision or rollback based on comparable evidence. A reusable amendment changes the canonical policy through normal review; skills continue referencing it. Return only material findings, next actions and genuinely required decisions. If no change is warranted, say so without manufacturing process work.

Apply the [communication and follow-through protocol](../../../docs/studio/POLICY.md#communication-and-unattended-follow-through). Check that material department transitions have a receiver acknowledgement and next action, and reconcile the producer queue to current owner evidence. Use authorized messages and existing coordination wakes to close missing receipts; distinguish an unresolved handoff from ordinary active review. Route routine owner decisions within delegated scope, reserving genuinely undelegated decisions for the product owner.
