# Process improvement record

Use with the [studio policy](../POLICY.md). Completed observations and trials stay local. Publish only a public-safe reviewed amendment.

- **Problem and evidence:** affected outcome, observed delay/rework, source timestamps and baseline; distinguish normal wait or stale observation.
- **Hypothesis:** suspected cause, uncertainty and alternatives.
- **Proposed amendment:** one bounded change, affected workflow and accountable owner.
- **Authority:** existing delegated authority or required department/product-owner decision; approved, pending or rejected, with evidence. No approval inferred from silence.
- **Trial:** eligible future package(s), start/stop conditions, effort/resource impact and preserved gates.
- **Success measure:** comparable before/after evidence, expected improvement and unacceptable side effects.
- **Rollback:** trigger, owner and restoration action that preserves work/evidence.
- **Result:** measured outcome, failures/rework, limitations and adopt/revise/rollback decision.
- **Policy disposition:** reviewed change reference or explicit no-policy-change; no whole-game acceptance credit.
